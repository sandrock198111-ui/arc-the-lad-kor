V329 cold-boot runtime checklist

1. Close the running game and boot 03_output/V329.cue.  Do not resume a V327
   or V328 emulator savestate because it contains the old EXE in RAM.
2. Load screen: confirm level and play-time rows show compact digits and blanks.
3. Enter the game and read normal dialogue containing 다/그/하/여/것/변.  These
   must remain correct Korean; V326-V328 could corrupt these one-byte codes.
4. Item screen: confirm item names remain Korean, their trailing quantities are
   compact digits, and no previous-looking Hangul glyph remains in blank space.
5. Battle/status screens: confirm HP, MP, level, top counters and compact symbols.
6. Confirm E7 button/orb icons still appear.
7. Choice rows may still begin with visible "다다".  Disk bytes show E5 03
   followed directly by option text; the two physical-0 packets are generated
   by the E5 control path and are intentionally outside V329.

Please capture fresh V329 states from at least:
- the load screen,
- an item/quantity screen,
- a battle or status screen,
- a normal dialogue and a choice screen if convenient.
