- Accept: 시작한다

Structural rule
- Never replace a choice body with plain text lines.
- Start from the untouched Japanese body.
- Preserve both E5 commands and all E6 commands at their original offsets and values.
- Replace only:
  1. the first two bytes with an E2 prompt reference,
  2. the two-byte first-option span after the first E5,
  3. the two-byte second-option span after the second E5.
- The second option retains five original tail bytes, so its E2 slot metadata must
  skip exactly 5.
- Prompt skip metadata must be derived from each body's original first E6 position;
  it is not a universal constant.
