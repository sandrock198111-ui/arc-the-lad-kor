V334 cold-boot checklist

IMPORTANT
- Do not load any V333 emulator savestate. It restores the bad CPU/RAM state.
- Boot V334.cue from power-off/cold boot.
- Memory-card saves are allowed; emulator save states are not.

Required checks
1. BIOS, title, load screen, and field gameplay all appear; no black screen.
2. Load screen shows slot number, L/level, and play-time digits correctly.
3. Choice rows have no synthetic `다다` prefix.
4. Item quantities and battle HUD L/M/P, HP and MP digits are compact and correct.
5. Battle/field icons remain visible.
6. Skill names and configuration text/bar retain the V332 left alignment.
7. Dialogue, item names, speaker labels, and ordinary Hangul remain unchanged.

Until all checks pass, V334 remains TEST_ONLY and bible_current.txt stays unchanged.
