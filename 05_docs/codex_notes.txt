S1021 live-runtime proof and control_0000 format - 2026-07-11
- During the house scene, DuckStation mapped PS1 RAM matched `1/S1021.DAT` at relation `RAM offset = file offset + 0xCF000`.
- Exact comparison was 305115/305152 bytes (99.9879%); four small runtime-mutated ranges account for the difference.
