Rules confirmed by testing
- Do not use 0x08-0x64 low-slot Hangul glyphs as a distribution approach without a separate controlled proof.
- Do not use E0-FF as general Hangul single-byte codes.
- Do not fill remaining text block space with 0x00.
- Do not patch visible pointer-looking or control-looking blocks by guess.
- Before any new mixed-text fix, identify the actual file and block used by that screen.
